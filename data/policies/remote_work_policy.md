# Remote Work Policy

**Company:** ExampleCorp | **Owner:** Human Resources | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Eligibility

### 1.1 Who can work remotely
An employee can work remotely after completing 6 months of service, if the line manager approves and the role allows it.

## 2. Remote Days

### 2.1 Number of days
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. The days must be agreed with the line manager in advance.

## 3. Availability

### 3.1 Core hours
Remote employees must be reachable on chat and email during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00.

## 4. Equipment and Allowance

### 4.1 Equipment
The company provides a laptop. Employees must use it for all company work.

### 4.2 Internet allowance
Employees with approved remote days receive an internet allowance of PKR 3,000 per month.

## 5. Security

### 5.1 VPN and devices
A VPN is required to access company systems when working remotely. Employees must not store company files on personal devices.
