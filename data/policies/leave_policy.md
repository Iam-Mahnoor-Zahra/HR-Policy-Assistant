# Leave Policy

**Company:** ExampleCorp | **Owner:** Human Resources | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy explains the types of leave available to all full-time employees of ExampleCorp and how to request them.

## 2. Annual Leave

### 2.1 Entitlement
Every full-time employee receives 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year (1 January to 31 December).

### 2.2 Applying for annual leave
Employees must request annual leave at least 3 working days in advance through their line manager. Leave of more than 5 consecutive days should be requested at least 2 weeks in advance. Approval depends on team workload.

## 3. Sick Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement
Every full-time employee receives 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

### 3.2 Medical certificate
A medical certificate is required when sick leave covers 3 or more consecutive days. The certificate must be given to HR within 2 days of returning to work.

### 3.3 Informing the manager
Employees should inform their manager as early as possible on the first day of sick leave.

## 4. Carry-Forward and Encashment

### 4.1 Eligibility
Only annual leave can be carried forward. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

### 4.2 Carry-forward limit
Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March of the next year, or they expire.

### 4.3 Encashment
Unused leave days cannot be exchanged for cash. Employees are not paid for unused leave days.

## 5. Maternity and Paternity Leave

### 5.1 Maternity leave
Female employees are entitled to 12 weeks of paid maternity leave. They should inform HR as early as possible before the expected start date.

### 5.2 Paternity leave
Male employees are entitled to 5 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.

## 6. Unpaid Leave

### 6.1 Unpaid leave
After using their annual leave, employees may request up to 10 days of unpaid leave per year. It needs approval from both the line manager and HR.
