# Code of Conduct

**Company:** ExampleCorp | **Owner:** Human Resources | **Version:** 1.0 | **HR Email:** imahnooriqbal@gmail.com

## 1. Respectful Workplace

### 1.1 Expected behaviour
All employees must treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect and professionalism.

## 2. Harassment and Discrimination

### 2.1 Zero tolerance
ExampleCorp does not tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination of any kind.

### 2.2 Reporting concerns
Concerns must be reported to HR by email at hr@examplecorp.com. HR keeps reports confidential as far as possible.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest and Gifts

### 3.1 Conflicts of interest
Employees must tell their line manager about any situation where personal interests could conflict with company interests.

### 3.2 Gifts
Gifts worth more than PKR 5,000 must be declared to HR.

## 4. Confidentiality

### 4.1 Confidential information
Employees must not share confidential company or customer information with anyone outside the company.

## 5. Use of Company Resources

### 5.1 Company equipment
Company equipment and systems are mainly for work purposes.
