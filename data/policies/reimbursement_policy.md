# Reimbursement Policy

**Company:** ExampleCorp | **Owner:** Finance and HR | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Eligible Expenses

### 1.1 What can be claimed
Employees can claim travel, accommodation and meals during approved business trips, client meeting costs that were approved in advance, and work-related training fees approved by the line manager.

## 2. Limits

### 2.1 Meals
Meals are reimbursed up to PKR 3,000 per day during business trips.

### 2.2 Accommodation
Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to PKR 20,000 per night.

### 2.3 Local transport
Local transport is reimbursed at actual cost, with receipts.

## 3. Submitting a Claim

### 3.1 Deadline
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

### 3.2 Documents
Each claim needs itemised receipts and an expense form approved by the line manager.

## 4. Processing

### 4.1 Payment time
Approved claims are paid within 10 working days.

## 5. Not Reimbursed

### 5.1 Exclusions
Personal expenses, traffic fines, alcohol, and any expense without a receipt are not reimbursed.
