# Attendance Policy

**Company:** ExampleCorp | **Owner:** Human Resources | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Working Hours

### 1.1 Standard hours
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00, including a one-hour lunch break.

## 2. Late Arrival

### 2.1 Grace period
Employees have a grace period of 15 minutes after 9:00. This can be used up to 3 times per calendar month.

### 2.2 Further late arrivals
Each late arrival beyond 3 in one month is counted as a half day of annual leave.

## 3. Absence

### 3.1 Notification
If an employee cannot attend work, they must inform their line manager by 10:00 on the same day.

### 3.2 Absence without notice
Absence without notice for 3 or more consecutive working days is treated as a serious matter and is handled directly by HR.

## 4. Overtime

### 4.1 Approval
Overtime must be approved by the line manager in advance.

### 4.2 Compensation
Approved overtime is compensated with time off in lieu, taken within 30 days.
